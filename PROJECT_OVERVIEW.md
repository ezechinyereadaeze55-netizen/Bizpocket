BizPocket — Business, Beautifully Managed.

About the Project

BizPocket is a business management application designed to help entrepreneurs and small business owners manage their daily business activities from one place.

The platform brings together essential business tools, making it easier to monitor sales, manage inventory, track expenses, organize customers, and manage invoices.

Key Features

- Business Dashboard: View important business information in one place.
- Sales Management: Record and monitor business sales.
- Inventory Management: Keep track of products and stock.
- Expense Tracking: Monitor business expenses.
- Customer Management: Organize customer information.
- Invoice Management: Create and track invoices and payments.
- Reports: Review business performance.
- Multiple Currencies: Supports NGN, GHS, USD and GBP.

Technology Stack

- React
- TypeScript
- Vite
- Tailwind CSS
- Lucide React
- AppDeploy backend and authentication services
